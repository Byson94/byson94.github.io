---
title: Departure of Sight
category: poetry
collection: Marginalia
date: 2026-11-22
---

A golden thread strikes my gentle face,
Awakening me for a tardy time of bliss.
The silver sun shines with lovely grace,
For it is a time that is not to miss.

The valley of flowers all bloom at once,
And dances in rhythm of the wind that rush.
The army of the green men take their stance, 
Preparing for an event that is new and fresh.

In a world where the soaring sky is always blue,
This event marks the beginning of someting new.
Retreating away are the tentacles of orange hue;
Fading away from our gazing view.

Little did we know this is the nearing end;
The eyes of heaven were the source of vision.
Into the ocean does all light descend,
Leaving us all in the worst position.
