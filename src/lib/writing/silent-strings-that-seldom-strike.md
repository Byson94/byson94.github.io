---
title: Silent Strings that Seldom Strike
category: poetry
collection: "Green Poetry"
date: 2026-11-21
---

Falling strings hit the daring dome above,
Knocking for someone it for long has known.
I sharpen my ears with fresh flowing love,
A gratitude for the kindness it had once shown.

Back when I had no dime to spare,
And was stranded afar like an old chair,
Fell on me the silver pearls with care;
Guiding me across for it is fair.

The silent splatter of the singing string,
Fulfills my destiny long lost at last.
Following the tune of the pearl I sing,
Take me away from this violent past.

A thousand constellations and one star,
Twinkle at the tune of the crying sky.
It revealed a path that lay afar;
That I walked across with an open eye.

Now I close my eyes in precious peace,
One gifted to me by the path unveiled,
With a penny and a shelter to my name;
I rest hearing the beats begin.
